Our Story

Poured by hand,
one jar at a time

Ember & Oak started in a Portland kitchen in 2019 with a single pouring pot and a stubborn belief: a candle should smell like the thing it's named after — not like a chemical approximation of it.

Every candle is still poured in small batches of 40, cured for two weeks, and tested by burn before it ever reaches a box. We use 100% soy wax, phthalate-free fragrance, and cotton wicks. Nothing you can't pronounce.

  • Small batches of 40 — never mass produced
  • Two-week cure for a stronger, cleaner scent throw
  • Reusable glass jars — return one, get 15% off
